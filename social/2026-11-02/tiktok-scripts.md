## Script 1: "Your 2027 budget is already written" (~40s)

**Hook (0–2s):**
VO: "Stop. Don't make a 2027 budget yet."
Screen: blank budget spreadsheet, cursor blinking.

**Body (2–30s):**
VO: "Your budget already exists. It's called your 2026 spending. Here's the move: pull the last 12 months from every account you spend from. Three buckets. Fixed — rent, insurance, loans. Those carry over. Variable — groceries, gas, eating out. Budget the 12-month average, not your best month. And irregular — car registration, gifts, repairs, annual renewals. Total a year of those."
Screen: Tally screen recording — accounts syncing, expense breakdown report, categories sorting, the yearly total appearing.

**Payoff (30–40s):**
VO: "Most people find hundreds a month hiding in 'one-offs.' Divide that total by twelve, fund it monthly, and next December isn't an emergency. Build the year first. Then let the months be different — because they are."
Screen: annual budget view, months with different totals.

**On-screen text:** "Your 2026 spending = your 2027 budget draft"
**Caption:** November beats January for budget season. Your accounts already know the numbers. #budgeting #personalfinance #2027budget
