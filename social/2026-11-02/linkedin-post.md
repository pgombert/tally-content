Unpopular opinion: January is the worst month to make a budget.

By January you're guessing. In November, you're not — you have ten months of real 2026 spending sitting in your accounts, and that record is the most honest budget draft you'll ever get.

I just published a step-by-step for building a 2027 annual budget out of your actuals. The parts people tend to miss:

Irregular expenses aren't surprises, they're unscheduled. Car registration, gifts, annual renewals, repairs — total a year of them and most households find several hundred dollars a month hiding in "one-offs." Divide by twelve, fund it monthly, and December stops being an emergency.

Variable categories get your 12-month average, not your proudest month. Budgeting $550 for groceries that average $740 is how budgets die in February.

And a few 2027 numbers are already set: Social Security benefits rise 3.6% in January, and HSA limits move to $4,500 self-only and $9,000 family. The 2027 401(k) and IRA limits land mid-November — if you budget to max out, wait for them before you lock that line.

Build the year first. Let the months be different from each other. Schedule the review.
