## Script 1 — "The $84,600 line" (~40s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of a simple on-screen text card "$84,600", then cut to a marketplace premium quote screen, then Tally's what-if scenario view showing income changing.

**VO:**
(0–2s) "One dollar of income can cost you thousands. Here's the line."
(2–10s) "The enhanced ACA subsidies expired in December. For 2026, the old cliff is back: about $62,600 of income if you're single, $84,600 for a couple."
(10–20s) "Under the line, your benchmark health premium is capped — at most about 9.96% of your income. For a couple, that's roughly $8,400 a year, max."
(20–30s) "One dollar over the line? The cap is gone. Not reduced — gone. You pay full sticker price, which in your sixties can be a multiple of that."
(30–40s) "If you retire before 65, your income is a dial — withdrawals, conversions, capital gains. Know where the line is before you set it. Full 2026 playbook is on the blog — link in bio."

## Script 2 — "Two kinds of income" (~35s)

**Visual:** Split-screen text: "Counts" / "Doesn't count" lists appearing line by line, then Tally forecast screen.

**VO:**
(0–2s) "Retired before 65? The government doesn't count all your income the same way."
(2–12s) "Counts toward the ACA cliff: IRA withdrawals. Roth conversions. Capital gains. Dividends. Interest."
(12–22s) "Doesn't count: Roth withdrawals. Spending your cash. And when you sell investments — only the gain counts, not the whole sale."
(22–30s) "So a couple spending $90,000 a year can show $60,000 of income — or $110,000. Same lifestyle. Very different health-insurance bill in 2026."
(30–35s) "Which accounts you spend from is a decision. Plan it. Link in bio."
