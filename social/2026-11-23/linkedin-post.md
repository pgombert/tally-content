There's a milestone on the way to retirement that almost nobody notices when they cross it.

It's the day your retirement savings become large enough that compounding alone — no further contributions — will carry you to a fully funded retirement on schedule. The FIRE community calls it Coast FIRE.

The math is one line. Take your retirement number (25x expected annual spending is the usual shortcut) and discount it back to today at a real growth rate. Targeting $1.5M at 65, assuming 5% real growth, a 45-year-old needs about $565,000 invested today. A 35-year-old needs about $347,000. A 55-year-old needs about $921,000. Same destination — the price difference is the compounding years you either have or don't.

Here's what I tell people before they act on that napkin math: the one-line formula ignores taxes, ignores Social Security, ignores market sequence risk, and takes your spending as a guess. $1.5M in a traditional 401(k) is not $1.5M you can spend. A Coast FIRE number that skips taxes can be overstated by six figures.

So run the simple math for motivation. Run the full model — real accounts, real tax treatment, Monte Carlo — before you change your life.
