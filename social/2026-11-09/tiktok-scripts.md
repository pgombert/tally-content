## Script 1 — "The question your budgeting app can't answer" (~40s)

**(2–8s)** Screen: a budgeting view, categories neatly filled. VO: "It knows your grocery average. It knows you overspent on dining in August. Now ask it: when can I stop working?"

**(8–14s)** Screen: scroll to an investment account shown as a single balance. VO: "Silence. In most budgeting apps your 401(k) is one number — the balance. That's it. No plan attached."

**(14–28s)** Screen: switch to Tally's forecast view; show the runway date, then the net-worth curve across decades. VO: "This is the same spending data with the second half attached. That date is called your runway."

**(28–36s)** Screen: open a what-if scenario, change one input (retire two years earlier), show the runway move. VO: "And it's not frozen. Retire two years earlier — watch the runway move. That's a question answered in ten seconds that most people pay an advisor to estimate."

**(36–42s)** End card: "Track today. Forecast the future." VO: "Fourteen-day free trial, no credit card. Link in bio."
