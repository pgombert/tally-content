## Script 1: "The month that kills every budget" (~40s)

**HOOK (0–2s)** — VO over a budget screen scrolling fast:
"Your budget isn't failing in the month you think it is."

**(2–10s)** — Screen: a clean monthly budget, everything green.
VO: "Here's a budget doing great. Four months, all green. Then March happens — car insurance, property tax, and a vet bill, all in one week."

**(10–20s)** — Screen: the March view, big red overspend.
VO: "A monthly budget has no memory. It can't tell the difference between you overspending and an annual bill showing up exactly on schedule. So March looks like a failure. Every single year."

**(20–33s)** — Screen: switch to Tally's annual budget view, scroll across 12 months, land on March showing the premium as planned.
VO: "The fix: budget the year, not the month. Take every bill that isn't monthly — insurance, taxes, registrations — put it on the year, and place it in the month it actually hits. Now March isn't a blowup. March is the plan."

**(33–40s)** — Screen: budget-vs-actuals, on plan.
VO: "That $1,200 premium? It was never a January problem. It's a hundred bucks a month that gets collected once. Budget it that way."

**On-screen end card:** "Budget the year. thetally.io"

## Script 2: "You have no idea what your subscriptions cost" (~35s)

**HOOK (0–2s)** — VO, black screen with big text "$86?":
"Guess what you spend a month on subscriptions. Go ahead."

**(2–12s)** — Screen: text flips from "$86" to "$219".

VO: "The worst offenders are annual renewals. You see them once a year, forget them for twelve months, and they auto-renew forever. The only fix is a list you didn't make from memory — pull it from your actual transactions."

**(24–33s)** — Screen: Tally subscriptions report, then a cancel-worthy line item highlighted.
VO: "Every recurring charge, from your real accounts, in one report. Ten minutes, once a quarter. Cancel what you don't recognize."

**On-screen end card:** "Find yours. thetally.io — 14-day free trial, no card."
