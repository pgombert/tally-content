## Script 1 — "The Secret Third Paycheck" (~40s)

**Format:** Screen recording of a calendar app + Tally cash planning view, voiceover.

**HOOK (0:00–0:02)** — On screen: October calendar with 2, 16, 30 circled.
VO: "Half of America gets a secret third paycheck in October. Check your dates."

**(0:02–0:10)** — Zoom on the circled Fridays.
VO: "If you're paid every other Friday and your paydays are October 2nd, 16th, and 30th — that's three paychecks in one month. It happens twice a year, because 26 paychecks don't fit into 12 months."

**(0:10–0:22)** — Cut to a notes app, typing a two-line plan.
VO: "Here's the problem. A paycheck without a job gets absorbed. You will not remember what you spent it on — I speak from experience. So before it lands, write the standing order: emergency fund until it's full, then the credit card, then invest."

**(0:22–0:35)** — Screen recording: Tally what-if scenario, runway date shifting.
VO: "And if you want motivation — this is what happens when you model investing both extra checks every year against your lifetime forecast. That date on the screen is when your money runs out. Watch it move."

**(0:35–0:42)** — End card: "3 paychecks. 1 plan." + thetally.io
VO: "Three paychecks, one plan. The full biweekly budgeting system is on the blog — link in bio."

## Script 2 — "Why Your Budget Is Always 3 Days Off" (~35s)

**Format:** Screen recording alternating a monthly budget spreadsheet vs. Tally cash planning timeline, voiceover.

**HOOK (0:00–0:02)** — On screen: spreadsheet that says "BUDGET: FINE ✓" next to a bank balance at $12.
VO: "Your budget says fine. Your bank account says twelve dollars. Here's why."

**(0:02–0:12)** — Highlight income row vs. bill due dates.
VO: "You're paid every two weeks. Your bills are monthly. Those two cycles drift — the check that covered early March lands three days late for early April. The monthly budget averages that away. Your checking account doesn't."

**(0:12–0:24)** — Screen: assigning bills to paychecks, then a due date being changed on a biller site.
VO: "Two fixes. Give each paycheck a job — check one covers housing, check two covers everything else. And move your due dates. Most billers will shift them if you ask, so cluster bills a few days after payday."

**(0:24–0:32)** — Screen: Tally cash planning, shortfall alert flagged on a specific date.
VO: "Then stop watching months and start watching dates. This view projects every payday and every bill forward and flags the exact day you'd go under — weeks early."

**(0:32–0:35)** — End card: thetally.io
VO: "Budget on the grid you're actually paid on. Link in bio."
